PATTERN 02 Conversational

Onboard, Don't Program

Treat AI like a new team member, not a search engine. The investment in context at the start pays forward across every interaction in the session.

Context

When this pattern applies

Extended AI-assisted work sessions where the model needs to understand your context, role, constraints, and preferences before producing useful output. Applies at the start of any project, engagement, or recurring workflow where the AI will be a consistent collaborator.

Problem

Every interaction starts cold

Users treat AI like a search engine — one prompt, one answer, repeat. Each interaction starts cold. The model has no context about who you are, what you're building, what constraints you operate under, or how you make decisions.

Result: generic output, repeated corrections, and constant re-explanation of things that shouldn't need re-explaining.

Solution

Open with a structured context document

Open extended AI sessions the way you'd onboard a new team member. Provide a structured context document at the start. Five things to include:

  • Who you areRole, industry, experience level
  • What you're working onProject, goal, current state
  • ConstraintsBudget, timeline, technology stack, regulatory environment
  • PreferencesCommunication style, level of detail, terminology
  • Decision criteriaWhat does "good" look like in your context

This isn't programming or jailbreaking. It's professional context-setting. The model produces work that fits your actual situation instead of a hypothetical average one.

Value Consequence

Output arrives already calibrated

Outputs arrive already calibrated to your context. The quality gap between "what AI produces" and "what I actually need" shrinks dramatically.

Less time correcting; more time building. The investment in onboarding pays forward across every subsequent interaction in that session.

Notes

Scales to teams

This pattern scales into persistent system prompts for teams and organizations. A shared onboarding document becomes a team's shared AI context — reducing variance in outputs and ensuring every team member's AI sessions start from the same foundation.
