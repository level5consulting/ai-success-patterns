Why this library exists

In 1994, Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, and John Vlissides published Design Patterns — not to invent new programming concepts, but to name and codify patterns that experienced developers were already using intuitively. Singleton. Observer. Factory. Decorator. Those words became the common language of software engineering for thirty years.

We are at that exact inflection point with AI. The patterns exist. The industry does not yet have the vocabulary. This library is the beginning of that vocabulary — built in public, released into the public domain, open to every practitioner who has a pattern to contribute.
