The ore-and-gold idea is inspired by Kent Beck's thinking on how we measure AI productivity.

PATTERN 06 In Review

AI as Amplifier, Not Foundation

Practice

AI makes you more of what you already are. If you bring expertise, it multiplies your output. If you bring a gap, it fills it with something that sounds like expertise - until someone asks a follow-up question.

PATTERN 07 In Review

Practice

AI used in the articulation layer builds on your capability. AI used in the discovery and synthesis layer substitutes for it. The output may look identical. The person who produced it is not.

Why this library exists

In 1994, Gamma, Helm, Johnson, and Vlissides published Design Patterns - not to invent new programming concepts, but to name and codify patterns experienced developers were already using intuitively. Singleton. Observer. Factory. Decorator. Those words became the common language of software engineering for thirty years.

We are at that exact inflection point with AI. The patterns exist. The industry does not yet have the vocabulary. This library is the beginning of that vocabulary - built in public, released into the public domain, open to every practitioner who has a pattern to contribute.

AI brings the ability to automate to a whole new group of people. Patterns help them get up to speed quickly and safely.
