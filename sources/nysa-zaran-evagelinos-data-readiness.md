# Nysa Zaran Evagelinos - LinkedIn post on data as a core tenet of AI-readiness

Pasted by Greg, October 21, 2026. Saved word for word. Author: Nysa Zaran Evagelinos (Data Strategy and Value Realization, Research Director). Posted about October 20, 2026. Greg had a conversation in the comments (his comments to follow). Paraphrase only on the site.

I've come to the conclusion that the focus on data as a core tenant of AI-readiness is flawed in focus. 
A lot of the current advice promotes that broadly and autonomously consumed enterprise data layer will be the go-forward enabler of Enterprise AI. Not a single actual AI developer that I have spoken to wants that. Most business workflows don't need that. The architectural implications of constructing and maintaining it are so vast, so complex, and so in need of careful ongoing curation to maintain relevance and control risk, that an enterprise would need to have a clear ability to capitalize on that investment in order to justify it in the first place. There will be opportunities to capitalize on that, but it's not for everyone. If you don't agree, I'd love to chat. This is an honest call for conversation, with people that actually build or actually research.
