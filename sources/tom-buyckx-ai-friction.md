## Original post text

Don't use a bicycle if you only need to cross the street. Yet that is exactly what many teams are doing with AI right now.

What used to be a 20-second internal reply has turned into a 10-minute prompting loop. It looks polished, but the actual impact is zero.

I saw the exact same pattern with performance reviews. Team members would take three solid bullet points, run them through an LLM, and produce five verbose, jargon-heavy pages. It looked thorough, but it completely missed the point: genuine reflection and coaching.

As leaders, our job isn't just driving AI adoption; it's preventing AI friction.

Efficiency means using AI where leverage actually exists (deep research, account prep, scenario modeling), not using it as an automated layer of corporate noise.

What resources or habits have you used as a manager to create awareness on using AI in a way that truly drives impact?
