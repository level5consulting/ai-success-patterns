Understanding human capabilities and what it takes to optimize work for people and people for work is just beginning.

Personality, particularly trait activation theory. Epistemic beliefs. Relational reasoning. Regulatory focus. Temporal depth. Sensory gating. 

And those are just stable factors. Imagine if we started caring whether people wanted to slow down for a little bit or were ready to go faster. 

In a world where AI amplifies the cognition, intention, and judgment of the person using it, understanding what happens between people's ears matters a TON.

The future of work demands talent intelligence in a richer and more rigorous manner than we have attempted to date. For many industries, and for some portions of all industries, understanding and optimizing human capability is going to become one of the primary sources of competitive differentiation.
