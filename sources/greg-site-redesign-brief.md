# Site redesign brief - Greg Stellflue, October 19, 2026 (verbatim)

Checked in the repo, October 19, 2026: the site does NOT use Jekyll. No _config.yml or Gemfile; plain static HTML (index.html plus patterns/*.html), served by GitHub Pages with a CNAME.

---

You are redesigning the look and feel of aisuccesspatterns.com, a public
pattern library for AI practitioners. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.
[From the site: it appears to use Jekyll front matter - confirm in the repo.]

MANTRA
Make the complex simple. Judge every design decision against this.

THE PROBLEM TODAY
The site is utilitarian and the colors are boring. It is a lot of reading
with no imagery.

WHO USES IT - TWO PATHS
The goal is for leaders and builders to love using the site. There are two
separate ways people use it, and the design must serve both:

1. The encyclopedia user - has used the site before. They come back to find
   a pattern they already used, or to check whether a pattern exists for a
   problem they have now.
   [Synthesized - confirm: this path needs fast search and an easy way to
   scan the whole catalog.]

2. The browser - heard about this patterns idea and wants to get started.
   They should leave with 2-3 patterns they can use right away.

THE NEWCOMER'S STARTING PATH
Feature these three patterns as a 3-part story that goes from easy to
difficult:
  1. Avoid AI Slop
  2. Assemble the Team
  3. Ghost in the Workflow

BRANDING
The site is related to Level 5ive Consulting but must not look owned by
Level 5ive. Do not use the Level 5ive color palette. Give the site its own
identity. The existing footer credit and link to Level 5ive stay as they are.

DESIGN REFERENCES
- LinkedIn - simple
- Amazon - nothing beats it for shopping
- Epic MyChart - a good interface
[Synthesized - confirm: borrow the calm, card-based simplicity of MyChart
and the search-and-browse feel of Amazon.]

SCOPE
Nothing is sacred. Layout, structure, navigation, colors, typography,
imagery, and page organization are all open to change.

[From the site - current structure to work from:]
- Sections: Library, In Review, Backlog, About
- Patterns are numbered and tagged by type (Conversational, Agentic,
  Accuracy, Governance) and level (Foundation, Practice, Leadership)
- Each pattern follows Problem, Value, Context, Solution, Notes
- Library has a search box and filters
- Calls to action: Talk to Greg (LinkedIn), Book a meeting, Contribute
