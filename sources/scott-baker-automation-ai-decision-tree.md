## Transcription (text in the image)
Title: Automation & AI Decision Tree - Evaluate. Choose. Create Real Value. ("Better questions. Smarter choices. Greater value.")

1. Is the process worth automating? Performed frequently? Consumes significant time, cost or resources? Pain points (errors, delays, rework)? Can it be simplified or eliminated instead? - No: Do not automate. Low value or eliminate the process. Focus on simplification. "Just because we can automate it, doesn't mean we should."
2. Is the process deterministic? Structured, consistent inputs? Clearly defined rules? Predictable outcome? - Yes: Use traditional automation. Rule-based automation (e.g., RPA, workflows, system integrations). Simple, reliable, and cost effective.
3. Does the task require reasoning or interpretation? Unstructured data (text, documents, emails)? Contextual understanding or judgment? Multiple possible outcomes? - No: Consider structured automation with human input. Rules-based automation with human review for exceptions.
4. Can we measure and manage the risk? Test for accuracy and reliability? Impact of an incorrect result? Appropriate human oversight? Decisions and actions traced and audited? Data access and security manageable? - No: Not ready for AI automation. Address risk, governance, or data constraints first. Consider human-in-the-loop or a more limited use case.
5. Does the business value justify the complexity? Expected benefits (time, cost, quality, scalability) significant? Justifies development, integration, licensing and ongoing support? Simpler alternatives? - No: Reconsider or start smaller. Simpler solution, narrower scope, or pilot before full implementation.

Yes to all: AI is the right solution - now choose the right approach. Select the simplest approach that can reliably deliver the desired outcome.
- AI-assisted automation (augment people): summarize, classify, draft, analyze; AI provides insights or recommendations; humans make the decisions. Best for improving productivity and decision support. (People + AI, faster insights, better decisions.)
- Agentic AI (automate with agency): AI can plan, reason and take actions within defined boundaries; integrates with systems and tools; requires strong governance and monitoring. Best for complex, multi-step tasks with variable inputs. (AI agents, end-to-end execution, greater scale.)
- Key considerations for all approaches: define success metrics (time, cost, quality, user satisfaction); implement guardrails and human oversight; monitor, measure and continuously improve; ensure responsible and ethical use; maintain security, privacy and compliance.
