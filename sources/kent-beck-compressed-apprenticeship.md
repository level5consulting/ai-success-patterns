## What is known about the pattern

**Failure mode (from index.html backlog):**
Expertise that took senior practitioners years to develop stays locked in their heads. Junior team members wait for mentorship time that never comes. AI's capacity to accelerate learning is left unused while organizations treat it purely as a productivity tool.

**Category:** Conversational

**Core concept (reconstructed from memory):**
Using AI to compress the learning curve that traditionally required years of mentorship and apprenticeship. Instead of waiting for senior practitioner time, junior team members use AI to simulate the mentorship conversation - asking it to explain, push back, grade their thinking, and surface what they don't know they don't know.

**Kent Beck connection:**
Beck has written extensively on software craftsmanship and apprenticeship models (Extreme Programming, etc.). The post Greg shared applied this thinking to AI as a learning accelerator, not just a productivity tool.
