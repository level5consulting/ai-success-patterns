## Summary
- Next transformation removes the transactional layer instead of adding a system: first the ERP/CRM interface disappears, then the apps stop being the system of record; a governed data foundation plus AI agents take over.
- Since the first data warehouses (1988), people still observe reality and re-key it into software. Humans act as "middleware" between siloed systems.
- Bolting an agent onto siloed, ungoverned data automates confusion.
- Example: a field technician speaks an update; edge AI creates the inventory update and support ticket with no one logging in.
- Data flows through layers (raw history, validated, current state, business metrics). Controls (integrity, audit, SOX, retention, residency) move to an enterprise-owned governance plane, with lineage logs of each agent's identity, sources, and policy; agents propose, humans supervise exceptions.
- Ontology/semantic layer becomes the enterprise's own asset.
- Recommendations: communities of practice and data literacy; govern with security and architecture; design around data flows; data catalog as a product; price voice/AR interfaces in business cases.
- No quantitative data; timeline is the author's projection.
