Is anyone else experiencing "AI acceleration" at work?

We hear a lot about AI "slop" in the media, but I'm noticing something different in my day-to-day work. My colleagues and I are finding that AI assistants aren't dumbing us down - they're actually making our brains work harder.

Here's what I mean: when you ask a colleague to draft something, review a CV, or build a presentation, there's natural breathing room. You get time to think, refine your strategy, and plan your next move.

With an AI assistant? It's back with the output almost instantly. Which sounds great for productivity, and it is - but it also means you're immediately onto the next decision, the next task, the next question. There's no pause button.

The efficiency is real, but so is the mental intensity. Instead of fewer decisions, I'm making more of them, faster. My brain has honestly never been busier with creative and strategic thinking.

Anyone else feeling this? Is AI acceleration the flip side of the productivity gains we're all chasing?
