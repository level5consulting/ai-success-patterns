## Original post text

AI is a great reader and a risky copier.

I learned this the hard way with spreadsheets.

I asked AI to read a complex spreadsheet and summarize the key insights. It did this brilliantly - identifying patterns, surfacing anomalies, and explaining relationships between data points that would have taken me hours to find manually.

Then I asked it to copy specific data from that spreadsheet into a new format.

That's where things went wrong.

The AI reconstructed the data from memory rather than copying it precisely. Numbers were slightly off. Rows were reordered. Some entries were missing entirely. It looked right. It wasn't.

The lesson: AI summarizes by understanding. It reconstructs by remembering. Those are two very different things, and confusing them is expensive.

Use AI to read and explain. Use your normal tools - filters, queries, reports - to extract and copy.

Ask AI to explain. Don't ask it to copy.
