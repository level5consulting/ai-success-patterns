Small business - travel

Jen Vodenlich

Travel advisor, LuxRally Travel. She posts destination spotlights and travel deals of the day on Facebook.

The problem

Her posts looked like everyone else's AI posts - and Facebook said so. An October cruise post went up with an "AI content" label, an all-caps emoji hook ("10 NIGHTS. 5 CARIBBEAN DESTINATIONS. ZERO KIDS!") and a busy flyer. And her busiest month was coming: Black Friday sales.

The patterns she used

Not one pattern - a string of two.

In the chatAvoid AI Slop with an LLM Interview then Runs on its ownThe Night Shift

The interview gets her own knowledge and voice into the post, so it reads like her. The Night Shift drafts her content while she's away, so it's waiting in the morning - and she keeps the final check.

Before and after

In her words

"I really love this Night Shift thing that you taught me. I have it set up to help me with my workflow and it's amazing."

- Jen Vodenlich, LuxRally Travel

Value realized from pattern usage

Posts that sound like her, and content ready when she wakes up. A small business would otherwise hire a copywriter and a designer for that work.
