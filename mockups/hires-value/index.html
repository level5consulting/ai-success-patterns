Mockup, not on the site. Three ways the hires could look in a pattern's Value section. Assemble the Team shown. Only figures verified Nov 5.

Option A - one line in the Value list

Hard questions move earlier in the process, where answering them costs effort instead of credibility
Output that has been stress-tested is different from output that merely looks stress-tested. Measure it: track how much rework comes back after stakeholder review.
That distinction matters most when the stakes are highest - exactly when the default agreeable voice is most dangerous
The hires you don't need: this covers work you'd otherwise hire for - 4 or 5 reviewers, such as a project management specialist (US median pay $102,320), a financial analyst ($103,570) and a compliance officer ($80,730). The real review, by the people who can say no, stays with people.
