Conversational

5 patterns
01 · Conversational

Begin with the End in Mind

Define what "done" looks like before you describe what you want AI to do. A common source of wasted iterations isn't bad prompts - it's no destination.

Foundation02 · Conversational

Onboard, Don't Program

Treat AI like a new team member, not a search engine. The investment in context at the start pays forward across every interaction in the session.

Foundation05 · Conversational

Avoid AI Slop with an LLM Interview

Before you write a single word of your presentation or article, make AI interview you. The difference between AI-assisted and AI-generated is everything.

Foundation09 · Conversational

The Compressed Apprenticeship

A junior practitioner can now get the equivalent of a senior practitioner's undivided attention - on any topic, at whatever depth they need, without waiting for calendar availability that never comes.

Leadership In review14 · Conversational

Embed the Expert

Your best practitioner reviews a charter in ten minutes and finds the three problems everyone else missed. That judgment helps one project at a time, when they're free. Package what you can of it as a skill - a set of instructions an AI runs the way they would - and it reaches every project, every time.

Agentic

3 patterns
04 · Agentic

The AI Operations Manager

Define a recurring coordination role and assign it to AI as a persistent function - not a one-time task. The coordination tax on every knowledge worker's week drops substantially.

Practice08 · Agentic

Assemble the Team

One person using AI alone gets one perspective back - polished, agreeable, and oriented toward completing the request. The friction that would have sharpened the thinking never happens. Assemble the team before the work begins.

Practice15 · Agentic

The Night Shift

Most people only use AI while they're sitting with it, so AI moves at the speed of their calendar. Hand it the work that doesn't need you - research, checking, drafting, records - to run while you're away, and keep the decisions for the morning.

Accuracy

4 patterns
03 · Accuracy

Test Adversarially

After AI generates output you intend to use, prompt it to attack the output. But first: recognize that trusting an output and knowing it is valid are not the same thing. Fluency is not accuracy. The gap between those two is where AI-assisted errors live.

Foundation06 · Accuracy

AI as Amplifier, Not Foundation

AI makes you more of what you already are. If you bring expertise, it multiplies your output. If you bring a gap, it fills it with something that sounds like expertise - until someone asks a follow-up question.

Practice In review07 · Accuracy

Own the Thinking

AI used in the articulation layer builds on your capability. AI used in the discovery and synthesis layer substitutes for it. The output may look identical. The person who produced it is not.

Practice In review10 · Accuracy

Models Are Not Good at Math - But They Can Program

Asking an AI to calculate is the wrong move. Asking an AI to write code that calculates is almost always the right one. The distinction determines whether your numbers are trustworthy.

11 · Governance

The Productivity-to-Headcount Trap

AI saves time. That much is often measurable. What is not measurable yet - not until you redesign the work - is what the organization should actually do with that time. Skipping that step and going straight to headcount reduction is where the expensive mistakes happen.

Leadership In review12 · Governance

Ghost in the Workflow

The person who built your AI workflow understood it. They knew why the prompt was worded that way, what the agent was never supposed to touch, and which outputs needed a second look. Then they moved on, and the workflow kept running without them.

Leadership13 · Governance

Automate the Invisible

The first AI project usually lands on the most visible work - and too often on the moments where customers decide whether to trust you. Point it instead at the low-visibility, low-risk friction behind those moments, and give people more time for the work only people should do.
