# Handoff record - Pattern 15, The Night Shift (working title)

## Purpose
A pattern for delegating substantive AI work to run unattended (overnight or while you're away), with the human making the judgment calls afterward. The new way Greg and Claude started working the week of October 12, 2026.

## Idea providers
- Greg Stellflue - raised it October 18, 2026: "We are missing a pattern and it is a new way we are working." Top priority, first in the build queue.
- Name suggested by Claude; Greg confirmed the idea. Final name not decided.

## Feedback providers
- None yet.

## Sources
- None external. Real example: this library's first week (October 12-18, 2026) - see BACKLOG.md and records/overnight-*.md.

## Red-team reviews
- October 19, 2026 - Problem and Value, live (Assemble the Team: Rachel Okonkwo, CISO - adversarial; Dev Patel, engineering manager; Linda Marsh, PMO director; Sam Torres, solo consultant). All six accepted by Greg:
  1. "Nothing goes live" made a control: access only to sandbox, branch, or drafts folder; no write access to live systems (Rachel). Carried into Step 3.
  2. Batch-job analogy as credibility - engineers trust overnight work behind review gates (Dev).
  3. False binary softened - most people never try, often because there's nothing ready to hand off (Dev).
  4. "A full day now takes two hours" moved to the top of Value (Linda).
  5. No backlog to hand off added to the Problem as a cause (Linda).
  6. Usage-limit bullet made conditional on the tool (Sam).
- October 19, 2026 - Context and Solution, live (same team). All four accepted by Greg:
  1. Treat what the AI reads as data, not instructions; minimum access (Rachel) - in Step 3.
  2. "What it couldn't do, and why" mandatory in the morning summary (Dev) - Step 5.
  3. Start small: one task the first night, research or source-checking (Linda) - Context.
  4. Backlog and brief merged - a good backlog item is the brief (Sam). Steps renumbered 1-6.
