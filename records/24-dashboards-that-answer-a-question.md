# Handoff record - Pattern 24, Dashboards That Answer a Question

## Purpose
AI makes dashboards nearly free, so they fill with every countable number and answer nothing. Fix: name the decision and the person, list what would change it, give AI the decision not the data, check every number at the source, retire it when the question changes.
