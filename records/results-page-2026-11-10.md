## Should worked examples live here instead of on pattern pages?

Greg asked whether pattern worked examples, like the Onboard, Don't Program LinkedIn comment helper, should move to this Results page. **Recommendation: no - keep worked examples on the pattern page, and keep the Results page for other people's results.** A worked example teaches the steps, so it belongs next to the steps; a reader on the pattern page needs it right there. The Results page earns trust because every story is a real person, in their own words, who said yes - Greg's own examples would blur that and turn it into a portfolio. The bridge: when a Results story uses a pattern, that pattern page gets a one-line "See it in real life" link to the story, and a Results story can link back to the worked example that shows how. If Greg wants his own work on the page later, give it a separate, clearly labeled "How Greg uses them" section below the client stories, not mixed in.
