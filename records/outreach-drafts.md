## 1. Global AI Milwaukee - Sessionize submission
Call for speakers: https://sessionize.com/global-ai-milwaukee-chapter (open until May 31, 2027; monthly meetings, second Wednesday, Pewaukee)

**Session title:** Ore Is Not Gold: The Patterns That Turn AI Activity into Real Value

**Description (paste into Sessionize):**
A 10x increase in ore may or may not produce any more gold. That's where most AI programs are: measuring drafts written, code generated, and hours "saved," and calling it value. In McKinsey's 2026 State of AI survey, 80% of respondents said AI improved their productivity - but only 37% reported any impact on profit.

The people getting gold from AI aren't using better tools. They're using better patterns - repeatable ways of working that nobody had named yet. In this session, Greg Stellflue walks through three field-tested patterns from his open AI Success Patterns library (aisuccesspatterns.com):
- Avoid AI Slop - get AI to draw out your expertise instead of replacing it
- Assemble the Team - stress-test AI work with named perspectives before it reaches a decision
- Ghost in the Workflow - keep AI agents and prompts understandable after the person who built them moves on

For each, you'll see the failure it prevents and a copy-and-paste way to run it in any AI tool the next morning.

**Takeaways:** tell ore from gold; name what works so good AI practice spreads; start tomorrow with free, ready-to-use prompts.

**Level:** Introductory to intermediate. **Format:** 45-minute talk with live examples (can run 30). **Audience:** practitioners and leaders putting AI to work.

**Speaker bio:**
Greg Stellflue is the founder of Level 5ive Consulting and creator of AI Success Patterns, an open library of practices that turn AI activity into measurable value. He has spent more than 20 years in enterprise portfolio and program management, including 14 years at Johnson Controls and nearly seven at Planview, where he was the internal expert on its AI capability. He is the author of Journey to Value.
