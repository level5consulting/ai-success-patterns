## Round 1 - Problem and Value

**Brian (adversarial):** My board funds AI on adoption. If my people read "use AI less," usage drops and my budget goes with it. This pattern has to say clearly it is not anti-adoption - it's about where adoption pays. Lead with "point AI at the leverage," not "leave it alone."

**Rosa:** The performance review example is the strongest thing here, and it's buried in paragraph two. It's also the one with real harm: people being judged on prose they didn't write. Give it its own short paragraph and say what was lost - the manager's own judgment.

**Ethan:** Greg's carpenter story is perfect - use it as the opener. "When is a drill not a drill? When it's a hammer." It makes the point without sounding anti-AI: the carpenter used the tool in his hand, not the toolbox.

**Priya:** You describe the reader's cost - more to read, less signal - but only in one line. That's my whole day. Add the reader's side to Value: shorter messages get read and answered faster.

**Round 1 proposed changes:**
1. Reframe the lead and Problem so it's clearly about where adoption pays, not less adoption - "point AI at the leverage" first. (Brian)
2. Give the performance review its own paragraph and name what's lost: the manager's own judgment. (Rosa)
3. Open the pattern with Greg's carpenter story ("When is a drill not a drill? When it's a hammer."). (Ethan)
4. Add a reader-side Value line: shorter messages get read and answered faster. (Priya)
