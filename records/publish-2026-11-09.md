## LinkedIn post (one post, both patterns)

BOGO Monday. Two patterns, one theme this week: get challenged before it matters.

Own the Thinking. AI can help you say what you already worked out, or it can do the working out for you. The two look identical on the page. The difference shows up when someone asks you to defend it.

Ten People, One Idea. Give ten people the same AI and you get ten polished proposals making the same points. I call it the Stormtrooper Effect: polished, competent, identical. A room where everyone sounds the same isn't a room.

Use them together. Write down what you think before the AI sees it. Let AI widen the options. Then make the call yourself.
