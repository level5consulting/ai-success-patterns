# Taco Tuesday Cooking Show - weekly promo videos (draft, October 22, 2026)

Greg's call (October 22, 2026): a recurring cooking-show promo for "A Ten-Minute Taco 'bout AI." Real video of Greg (not AI-generated); AI makes the "dish" - real AI output that Greg tastes. Humor is self-deprecating. Wardrobe: Greg's signature red shiny coat (every episode).

## The fixed format (20-40 seconds, works muted - captions carry it)
1. **Open:** Greg at the counter in the red shiny coat, taco in hand. Caption: "Today on Taco Tuesday, we taste..."
2. **The dish:** the week's AI-made "dish," shown on a screen or held up (real AI output).
3. **The bite:** Greg takes a bite of the taco, looks at the dish, pauses.
4. **The verdict:** a self-deprecating line.
5. **Close:** "The secret ingredient was you." + "A Ten-Minute Taco 'bout AI - every Tuesday" + link.

Set: same counter, same phone position, same coat. Batch-record four at once.
