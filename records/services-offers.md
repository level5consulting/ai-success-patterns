**The promise in every offer (Greg, November 2, 2026):** give your team a common language for AI.

## Offer 1 - Starter Pack Setup ("Up and Running in a Week")
**For:** a team lead (PMO first) who downloaded a starter pack, or would like someone to do it for them.
**What's included:**
- A 30-minute kickoff: the team's work, tools (Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot) and one goal.
- The starter pack installed and tuned to the team's work and words (the Start-here skills).
- One 90-minute live working session: the team runs the first three patterns on real work.
- A one-page "how we use AI here" note, written from the session.
- Two weeks of email questions.
**What's not:** IT or security setup, licenses, custom integrations.
**Price:** $2,500 per team, fixed. Small-business version (owner plus 1-3 people - the travel agent, the nursery): $750.
**Pilot:** the first two small businesses at 50% off in exchange for a testimonial and a case study.

## Offer 2 - "Start Here" Half-Day Workshop
**Pitch:** your team leaves speaking the same AI language - five named patterns everyone can use in a sentence.
**For:** a leadership team, PMO, or association chapter (PMI, MMAC, Waukesha business groups).
**What's included:**
- A Taco 'bout AI-style live session: the Start-here path (05 -> 25 -> 15 -> 08 -> 12) run on the group's own work.
- Each person leaves with one pattern working on one real task.
- A short pre-session survey and a one-page summary afterward with the three patterns that fit this group.
- Optional add-on: the starter pack for their role.
**Price:** $4,000 for up to 20 people, onsite anywhere; travel and expenses billed at cost outside the Milwaukee area, capped at $2,000. Virtual: $3,500. (Greg, November 2, 2026: priced so people prefer onsite.) Association or chapter version: a 60-minute talk at a reduced fee or free, as a lead source.

## Value evidence for the ROI sheets (researched November 2, 2026 - verified on the publisher's own pages unless noted)
- **Rework (workslop):** BetterUp Labs with Stanford Social Media Lab, September 2025 (survey of US desk workers; BetterUp gives 1,004 and 1,150 in different places): 40% received workslop in the last month; 15.4% of work received is workslop; 1 hour 51 minutes to deal with each instance; $186 per employee per month; over $9 million a year for a 10,000-person organization. https://www.betterup.com/blog/hidden-costs-workslop ; HBR, Sept 22, 2025: https://hbr.org/2025/09/ai-generated-workslop-is-destroying-productivity
- **Lost knowledge:** Panopto Workplace Knowledge and Productivity Report (2018, 1,000 respondents): 42% of institutional knowledge is unique to the individual; knowledge workers lose 5.3 hours a week waiting for or recreating information; large US companies lose $47M a year. (via HR Dive, July 18, 2018) https://www.hrdive.com/news/inefficient-knowledge-sharing-costs-large-us-businesses-47m-a-year/527892/ . Turnover cost: Gallup, half to two times salary (already cited on Pattern 12).
- **Existing license usage:** Gartner 2025 Microsoft 365 and Copilot survey (187 IT leaders, May 2025, via techpartner.news June 26, 2025): 40% piloting, only 5% of those finished with pilots moving to larger deployment; nearly half rated it "some value, shows promise"; Microsoft's default counts one use in 28 days as "active." Gartner Oct 28, 2025: 88% of HR leaders say no significant business value from AI tools; only 42% of employees know where AI can improve their work. https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-10-28-gartner-survey-shows-88-percent-of-hr-leaders-say-their-organizations-have-not-realized-significant-business-value-from-ai-tools . No verified primary source found for "% of paid Copilot seats sitting idle."
- **Failed transformations / no roadmap:** RAND, Aug 13, 2024: more than 80% of AI projects fail (twice non-AI IT); most common cause is misunderstanding the problem; 84% of interviewees cited leadership-driven causes. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA2680-1.html . BCG, Oct 24, 2024 (1,000 executives): 74% have no measurable value; 70% of challenges are people and process. https://www.bcg.com/press/24october2024-ai-adoption-in-2024-74-of-companies-struggle-to-achieve-and-scale-value . Gartner, July 29, 2024: at least 30% of GenAI projects abandoned after proof of concept by end of 2025 (unclear business value among reasons). MIT NANDA, The GenAI Divide, July 2025: 95% of organizations see no measurable P&L return; only 5% of custom tools reach production (via Virtualization Review).
