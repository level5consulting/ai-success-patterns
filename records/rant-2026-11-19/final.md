Here's my entire AI Success Patterns library as a cheat sheet. Every pattern, 165 commands, the services, the starter packs, a bonus section. I made it myself. I wrote every word on it, and I still wouldn't read it. That's why it says DO NOT DO THIS.

AI cheat sheets show up in my feed every week. Small type, lots of colors, "save this for later." If one got you started, keep it. Just don't expect your team to run on it.

Here's the problem. Nobody brings tip 37 to Monday's meeting. Shortcuts only you have memorized aren't shortcuts. They're a private language, and nobody else on your team speaks it.

The acronyms don't stick either. I've taken three beginner AI courses and learned three prompting acronyms. I remember zero of them. Yet Another Acronym No One Will Remember. YAANOWR for short.

What sticks is a name you can say out loud. Before the business case goes to the steering committee, someone asks, "Did you run Assemble the Team on that?" Everyone in the room knows what it means: name the CFO, the skeptical sponsor and the delivery lead, and hear their pushback before they do it live. That's not a tip. That's a common language.

So skip the cheat sheet. Start with five names. Avoid AI Slop with an LLM Interview: AI interviews you first, and your answers become the to-do list.
