# AI vendors and training on business data - checked November 10, 2026 (4 PM item, with Greg)
For the candidate pattern "Know Where Your Data Goes." Re-check before publishing anything; policies change.

- **Microsoft 365 Copilot:** "Prompts, responses, and data accessed through Microsoft Graph aren't used to train foundation LLMs, including those used by Microsoft Copilot." Page footer: last updated 2026-11-03. https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-365-copilot/microsoft-365-copilot-privacy
- **Google Workspace (Gemini):** customer content is "not human reviewed or otherwise used for Generative AI model training outside your domain without permission." https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub
- **OpenAI:** "We don't use content from our business offerings" (names ChatGPT Team, Enterprise, API Platform) for training by default; services for individuals "may use your content to train our models" unless you opt out. The page doesn't name the newer "Business" or "Edu" plan names. https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance
- **Anthropic:** "By default, we will not use your inputs or outputs from our commercial products" (Claude for Work, API, Claude Gov) to train models, unless you send feedback or otherwise allow it. Consumer plans (Free, Pro, Max) are covered by a separate article - not checked. https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996868-is-my-data-used-for-training

Green / yellow / red, as used with a small-business owner: green = nothing sensitive, start here; yellow = internal, business accounts only; red = payment, employee personal, banking/tax - never in AI. Unsure = next color up.
