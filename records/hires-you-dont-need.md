Others for later: writers and authors $76,910; public relations specialists $74,750; market research analysts $78,760; training and development specialists $69,280.

How to say it: "a role that pays a median of about $X" - pay only, not total cost (benefits add more; we have no verified figure for that yet). Never say the pattern replaces a person outright; say it covers the work you'd otherwise hire for.

## Two sources verified

- **Work AI Index 2026** - Glean's Work AI Institute, released June 10, 2026. 6,000 full-time digital workers (US, UK, Australia), surveyed Dec 2025 - Jan 2026.
