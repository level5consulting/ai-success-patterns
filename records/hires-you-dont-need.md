## Two sources verified

- **Work AI Index 2026** - Glean's Work AI Institute, released June 10, 2026. 6,000 full-time digital workers (US, UK, Australia), surveyed Dec 2025 - Jan 2026. AI saves about 11 hours a week; "botsitting" (making AI output usable) takes 6.4 hours a week, 37% of AI time. 87% use AI at work; only 13% say it significantly improved their organization's performance. https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260610538753/en/
- **Hannigan, McCarthy and Spicer (2024)**, "Beware of botshit: How to manage the epistemic risks of generative chatbots," Business Horizons 67(5), 471-486. DOI 10.1016/j.bushor.2024.03.001.
