# Handoff record - Pattern 20, Iterate to the Destination

## Purpose
Names the failure of exploring with AI with no written destination (circling) or a frozen one (a polished answer to the wrong question), and the fix: write the destination as a labeled guess, ask for options, rewrite the destination each round, keep the versions, then lock it and switch to Pattern 01.
