Bourbon Friday.

No pour this week.

I spent part of this week in Louisville. If you know bourbon, you know what that means. Distilleries down every road, bottle shops with things you never see up north, the Bourbon Trail practically calling my name.

I didn't buy a single bottle.

I was there to take care of family. And sometimes you need to set everything else down and focus on the most important things.

The bourbon will still be there next time. So will the work. This week, neither one was the point.

Back to the barrel next week.

Bringing back the past to help our future.
