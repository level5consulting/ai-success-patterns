# Story bank - LinkedIn comment helper

Greg's real stories for comments. **Never invent a story.** Use only what is here, or a story Greg names. Greg adds to it anytime. Keep it generic: no company or client names unless Greg says so. COBOL line at most twice a week (rotate Smalltalk, Java, "as a developer").

## New comment recipe (Greg, Nov 18, 2026)
- Every comment has one real story from this bank AND a pattern name.
- The pattern name comes up inside the story, the way Greg would say it out loud. Never "We call it X: [definition]." No neat closing line.
- Some comments push back on part of the post (agree with one point, disagree with another).
- Vary the shape: one line some days, three others; sometimes reply to another commenter, not the author; humor now and then.
- No questions to the author (Greg's pick). The helper still drafts.
- Why: people are noticing a pattern in AI-generated comments, and ours was close to it.
