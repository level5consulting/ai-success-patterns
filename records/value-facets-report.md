## Headline findings

1. **My hypothesis was half wrong.** I expected most bullets to be time and quality. In fact **risk reduction (13 bullets) and capability (11)** lead. The real gap is the one that matters to boards: **cost avoidance (5) and strategic value (4)** are the least covered facets - the board's two love languages, profitability and strategy, are the thinnest part of the library's value story.
2. **15 of 39 bullets can carry a verified published figure; 20 can carry a measure a reader runs in their own organization; 4 should stay qualitative.** Nothing needs an invented number.
3. **The best new evidence is the workslop research** (BetterUp Labs and Stanford Social Media Lab, Sept 2025). It quantifies Pattern 05's value, supports AI Friction, and fits the "ore is not gold" talk.
4. **Jeff's MIT and McKinsey ask is answerable now** - see the value-realization section below. Both say the same thing the patterns say: activity is not value.
5. **Three bullets overclaim** with words like "dramatically," "substantially," and "measurably" and no measure behind them (02, 04, 08).

## Value realization - Jeff's MIT and McKinsey ask

- **MIT NANDA, "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025"** (July 2025; review of 300+ public AI initiatives, 52 interviews, 153 leader surveys): despite $30-40 billion in enterprise GenAI spending, 95% of organizations report no measurable business return. Reported by Virtualization Review, Aug 19, 2025. **Caution:** the report's methodology is thin and the 95% is widely quoted out of context - use it as "MIT researchers found most organizations report no measurable return," not as a precise statistic.
- **McKinsey, "The State of AI in 2026: On the Road to ROI"** (survey May 4 - June 8, 2026; 1,719 respondents, 97 nations): 80% say AI improved their individual productivity, but only 37% report any EBIT impact, and about 6% are high performers attributing 5%+ of EBIT to AI. Nearly three-quarters of high performers fundamentally redesigned workflows, vs. about a quarter of everyone else. **Verified at the source PDF.**
- **Why it matters for the site:** McKinsey's 80% vs. 37% is "ore is not gold" in one line - individual productivity (ore) is not enterprise value (gold). It also supports Redesign the Stream (workflow redesign separates high performers) and Pattern 11.
