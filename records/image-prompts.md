# Image prompts - photorealistic theme (overnight, October 19-20, 2026)

For Greg to generate in his image tool. Claude places each image on its page with the alt text below.

**Style to match (from 01, 08 and 12):** photorealistic, modern office, natural window light, real professional people in business casual, shallow depth of field. AI shown as soft glowing screen content or translucent holographic overlays - never robots. A thought bubble is fine where it tells the story (08, 12). **No title text, captions or words in the image** - short screen labels only when the story needs them. Wide landscape format, about 16:9.

**Order suggested:** 13 first (publishes next), then 14 and 15 (newest In Review), then the redos (02-05), then the rest.

## 13 - Automate the Invisible (NEW - publishing next)
**Prompt:** Photorealistic image of a modern customer service area in natural light. In the foreground, a warm, unhurried conversation: a customer service rep smiles at a customer across a small table, fully engaged. Behind them, seen through a glass wall, a back office is shown as faint translucent holographic overlays - tangled lines of handoffs, stacked queues, forms being re-keyed, a clock icon over a waiting ticket - being quietly smoothed into clean, straight flowing lines by soft glowing AI elements. The front stays human; the AI works on the hidden friction behind it. Shallow depth of field, no text.
**Alt text:** A customer service rep has an unhurried conversation with a customer in the foreground. Behind a glass wall, glowing AI overlays straighten out the tangled handoffs and waiting queues in the back office - the friction customers feel but never see.
