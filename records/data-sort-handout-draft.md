# Green, yellow, red - sort your data before you hand it to AI (one-page draft)

**Draft 1 for Greg, Nov 18 night.** Optional handout for an in-person meeting (green / yellow / red data sort). Generic on purpose: it makes no claim about any vendor's terms - those must be checked on each vendor's own pages before anything is printed. Candidate pattern behind it: Know Where Your Data Goes (BACKLOG idea, Nov 10).

---

**The worry we hear most from owners isn't "will AI work?" It's "where does my data go?"** Start by sorting it. Ten minutes, one sheet, before anyone pastes anything.

**GREEN - go.** Information you'd be fine seeing on your website tomorrow.
Examples: published prices and product descriptions, job postings, your public policies, meeting agendas with no names, a blank template.
Use: any AI tool your company has approved.

**YELLOW - slow down.** Internal, but not harmful if it got out.
Examples: internal process notes, draft plans, de-identified numbers, meeting notes without customer or employee details.
Use: only a business plan whose terms say your data isn't used for training - check that in the vendor's own terms, not in a sales deck. Strip names first.

**RED - stop.** Anything that would hurt a customer, an employee or the business if it leaked.
Examples: customer and employee personal details, health or financial records, contracts and pricing under NDA, passwords and account numbers.
Use: keep it out of AI tools unless your company has a written decision, a signed agreement and someone who owns it.

**Three habits that make the sort stick**
1. Start with green work. The quickest wins are the invisible tasks nobody sees - and they rarely need yellow or red data.
2. When in doubt, call it the next color up.
3. Write the sort down and share it, so the whole team uses the same three words.
