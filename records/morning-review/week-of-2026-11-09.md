Step title: Prepare for the Night Shift - do the people parts before you leave.

With tonight's preview in front of you, go through the plan and find every step that would stop and wait for a person: an approval, a login, a site or tool the AI hasn't been allowed to use yet, a decision only you can make. Do those together in the afternoon, before the run starts, so the night runs with no one stepping in. A run that stops at 11 PM to ask a question sits idle until morning.
