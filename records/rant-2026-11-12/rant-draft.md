## Draft 1

Rant Thursday.

Every week another AI cheat sheet shows up in my feed. Two hundred tips on one page. Ninety-nine shortcuts to memorize. Tiny type, nine colors, "save this for later."

So I tried it with my own library. Image 1 is every AI Success Pattern crammed onto one page. 165 commands, three services, the starter packs, a bonus section. I wrote every word on it and I still wouldn't read it. I did laminate a copy. It's under my coffee.

Image 2 is how a cheat sheet actually works. It's an eye chart. Everybody reads the top line. By line 9 you're squinting, by line 12 you're guessing, and nobody has ever walked out of an eye exam remembering the bottom row.

That's where tip 37 and tip 112 live. They never make it to Monday's meeting. Neither do the acronyms. I've taken three beginner AI courses and learned three prompting acronyms. I remember zero of them. YAANOWR: Yet Another Acronym No One Will Remember.

And 99 shortcuts only you have memorized isn't a shortcut. It's a private language. Nobody else on your team speaks it.

What sticks is a name you can say out loud. "Did you run Assemble the Team on that before it goes to the steering committee?" Everyone in the room knows what that means. That's not a tip. That's a common language.

The top line of my eye chart is five names. That's the whole Start here path, and each one sets up the next. This week's is Assemble the Team: before the work goes out, name the people who will push back on it and hear them first. I ran it on this post. The team was not gentle.

Five names, one line each: aisuccesspatterns.com/#start

Bringing back the past to help our future.

#AI #AISuccessPatterns #Leadership
