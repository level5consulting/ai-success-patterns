# Handoff record - Pattern 26, Judge the Outcome, Not the Craft

## Purpose
AI can do work leaders can't do or check line by line. Lead it the way managers lead experts they can't replace: own the goal, judge outcomes not method, use independent and downstream review, separate the technical call from the business call, and avoid depending on one source you don't understand.
