**Celebrate the Win**

*When AI saves you time, nobody notices - including you. Decide up front what a win looks like, so you know when you hit one. Then stop and celebrate it with the people who made it happen.* [Synthesized]

**What goes wrong**
You start using AI and things get faster. But you never said what "better" would look like, so you never notice when you get there. I have no idea what milestones I've passed, because I never set any. The people who did the work don't get thanked - and some of them leave. I've watched it happen twice.

**What to do**
1. Before you start, decide what a win is. Your own goal - using 10 patterns, saving a set amount of time - or one your organization cares about, like growing without adding people. Write it down.
2. When you hit it, stop and say it out loud. What changed, and who did it. Use your own numbers - the free Value Worksheet can help.
3. Celebrate in a way those people would actually enjoy. If you don't know them well enough to pick something they'd value, a sheet cake won't fix that.

**An example**
My first manager couldn't take us out for a beer - half the team wasn't 21. So he took us to dinner. He made it about what we'd delivered for the customer, and since we were all new in town, he showed us the restaurants too. Small, and I still remember it.

**Why it matters**
Constant pressure wears people down. Celebrating a win is how they recover.

**When to use it**
When you hit a goal you set, and at any regular check-in you already have.
