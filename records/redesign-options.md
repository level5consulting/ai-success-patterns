# Site redesign options (overnight, October 20-21, 2026)

Mockups only - nothing on the live site changed. Files: mockups/option-{a,b,c}-home.html and -pattern-12.html, screenshots in mockups/shots/ (desktop 1366 wide, phone 390 wide). Built from the real 15 patterns and Pattern 12 content, using the photorealistic images. Each has working search and category filters. Own palette (not Level 5ive's); footer credit kept on every page.

All three serve both paths from the brief:
- **Newcomer path:** a 4-step "Start here" sequence - 05 LLM Interview, 15 The Night Shift, 08 Assemble the Team, 12 Ghost in the Workflow (the story: interview yourself, hand off the backlog, stress-test, make it outlast you). Proposed order - confirm.
- **Encyclopedia path:** search plus a full filterable index of every pattern.

## Option A - The Shelf (Amazon-style)
Search bar is the header. Start-here row with images, then one horizontal shelf per category, then an A-Z index. Pattern page: hero image, sections in the main column, a sticky "Use this pattern" box on the right (gold, the one bold color).
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque + Atkinson Hyperlegible. Palette: cool gray, ink, marigold.
- Best for: people who arrive with a problem and search. Scales well to 40+ patterns.
- Risk: busiest of the three; shelves need images to feel full.

## Option B - Care Plan (MyChart-calm)
Left rail navigation (Start here, All, By level, In review, Coming soon). Home is "Welcome. Here's your starting plan." - a numbered checklist of the four, then a Find-a-pattern list with category and level tags. Pattern page: section tabs and a prominent Use this pattern band near the top.
- Font: Figtree. Palette: deep teal, sky, coral for "In review."
- Best for: newcomers and anxious adopters (fits Pattern 16's people side). Feels like a guided program - good for webinar follow-up.
- Risk: least visual; images play a small role.

## Option C - Field Guide (editorial, photo-led)
Full-bleed photo hero with "The vocabulary AI practice is missing." and a search box. Numbered 1-4 trail with large images, then "The guide" as a clean table (number, pattern, type, level). Pattern page: long-read column, wide image, Use this pattern card at the top of the article.
- Fonts: Newsreader + Public Sans. Palette: white, ink, electric blue.
- Best for: credibility and speaking/consulting leads - looks like a publication, shows off the new images.
- Risk: needs an image on every pattern (06, 07, 09, 10, 11, 15 still pending).

## Recommendation
**Option C as the base, borrowing B's starting-plan checklist for the newcomer path.** C makes the strongest first impression for the people Greg wants to reach (event organizers, executives), and the photorealistic images are the site's best asset. The table index is the cleanest encyclopedia view. B's "starting plan" wording is warmer than C's trail for newcomers. Hold a rollout until the six missing images are done, or use a typographic tile for those.
