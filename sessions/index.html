Every Tuesday, one AI pattern in about ten minutes - the failure, the pattern run live, and a prompt you can use today.

Session 1 - October 27, 2026 - Pattern 05

Avoid AI Slop

Carla is a CEO with a board presentation in a week. Her AI tool writes it in ninety seconds - she spends three evenings editing it, and still can’t answer the board’s question about slide 12. That’s AI slop.

In this session, Greg Stellflue runs the fix live: don’t ask AI to write. Ask it to interview you first. Watch the pattern turn Carla’s own expertise into the source material - then use the same prompt in any AI tool today.

The prompt: Pattern 05, Avoid AI Slop with an LLM Interview - Use this pattern. Watch on YouTube: youtu.be/PUc2a4pwnPc
