## The cast (proposed - Greg's OK needed)
- **The leader (proposed): Carla Brennan**, CEO of a regional manufacturer (fictional), back for a fourth time. Since her board said yes, she has been told to "be more visible." Buyers and plant leaders should know how her company thinks. She has no time and hates sounding like a sales bot.
  - Why Carla: same leader across the series; viewers know her; you don't need the earlier sessions to follow.
  - Alternative: no persona - Greg plays himself, because the routine is really his. Stronger proof, but it makes the whole session about Greg's own LinkedIn (see open choice on the reveal in Act V).
- **The helper:** an AI chat Carla briefs once and uses every weekday. It drafts. She posts.

## The seven patterns (the string, in the order the routine uses them)
1. **Onboard, Don't Program** - a five-part briefing, played back before any work.
2. **The Chat Is Not Your Memory** - the routine lives in a record, not the chat. When a rule changes ("always get the post's link"), the record changes.
3. **Automate the Invisible** - the AI does the invisible work: search terms, the fit check, the drafts, the schedule. The comment is relationship work, so it stays hers.
4. **Own the Thinking** - she rewrites each draft with something only she knows.
5. **Right Tool, Right Job** - LinkedIn's own search, with the Past 24 hours filter, finds the posts. No bot ever touches her account.
6. **Ghost in the Workflow** - the helper has a written job description, so someone else could pick it up.
7. **The AI Operations Manager** - the helper is registered like a new hire, with a feedback field she fills in.

The loop (Greg's version only): the comments point people to the same patterns the routine is built on.
